# Task 2 (essay) guidance

- Introduction should paraphrase the prompt and state a clear thesis/position.
- Each body paragraph needs one central idea with a topic sentence and a developed example.
- The conclusion should restate the position without introducing new ideas.
- For opinion (agree/disagree) essays, the position must be unambiguous, not "it depends".
- For discussion (both views) essays, both sides must be covered before the writer's opinion.
- Aim for at least 250 words; award nothing extra for padding.
