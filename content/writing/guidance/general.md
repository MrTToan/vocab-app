# General grading guidance

_This file is injected into every writing score. Add your teacher's advice, formulas,
and rules here — the examiner will apply them. Keep it concise; edit freely._

- Reward a clear, consistent position that is maintained throughout.
- Value paragraphs that follow a **point → explain → example** structure.
- Penalise memorised, off-topic chunks that don't answer the specific prompt.
- Prefer precise, topic-specific vocabulary over vague words (thing, stuff, a lot).
- A wider range of accurate complex sentences lifts Grammatical Range; do not reward
  complex sentences that contain errors over simple sentences that are correct.
- Flag overused linking words (Firstly, Moreover, In conclusion) if they feel mechanical.
